    💧 HOW TO PLAY: FIX THE WATER PIPES!

    Help the Moon Base melt and filter lunar ice into clean drinking water! There is more than one way to connect the pipes—brainstorm the smartest route using the fewest turns!

    🔄
    1. Tap to Rotate

    Tap any pipe tile to turn it 90°. Connect the 🧊 ICE (Start) on the left to 💧 WATER (Goal) on the right.

    🧠
    2. Multiple Solutions!

    Each board has a Short Upper Route and a Longer Lower Route. Look at the starting pieces to pick the path that needs fewer turns!

    📈
    3. Fewer Turns = More +% Water

    Every extra rotation lowers your Efficiency %. Finishing at or near the Target Turns gives your base the highest +% Water boost!

    🛰️ Efficiency Tip:

    Before tapping, count how many turns the top row needs versus the bottom row. Choosing the path that is already mostly aligned saves turns and maximizes your water recovery!
